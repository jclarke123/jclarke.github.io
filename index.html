Curriculum Vitae (CV)

Hi! I’m Jonathan Clarke, a graduate researcher of dual UK/US nationality. I graduated from Durham University in 2021, and started my PhD under Prof. Dominik Riechers at the University of Cologne in 2022. I work with high-redshift galaxies and cosmological simulations, specifically in the area of sub-millimetre wavelength line intensity mapping (LIM).

As part of my PhD I am making simulations for expected observations to be made by the Fred Young Submm Telescope (FYST), part of the CCAT Observatory CCAT Observatory which I am affiliated with. In particular, I implemented models to emulate FYST’s upcoming EoR-Spec Deep Spectroscopic Survey, a LIM experiment spanning >8deg² that targets CO and [CII] up to z=8.

My work with line intensity mapping is especially novel in how it directly uses existing catalogue data in the targeted fields (e.g. COSMOS2020, Euclid) as the basis for the simulations. These simulations are especially useful as they reduce the need for underlying cosmological assumptions, whilst also accurately reflecting foibles in the fields which we will need to account for, such as overlapping target and interloper signal.

I primarily developed these tools for FYST's LIM observations, however I have recently branched out into other submm to optical wavelength experiments. These can be used to make cross-instrument correlation predictions, a particularly exciting area of research in LIM. Other simulated instruments include SUBLIME-TIFUUN, TIM, EXCLAIM, COMAP, and SPHEREx. I am also looking into expanding this research to 21cm, which is especially relevant considering recent detections with CHIME.

At present I am based at the University of Cologne, with a guest researcher position at the Max Planck Institute for Radio Astronomy (MPIfR), in Bonn. I am also affiliated with the CRC/SFB1601, and with the Dynaverse excellence cluster, research centers funded by the German Science Foundation (DFG).

Outside of work, I like to go on long hikes, I enjoy fencing, and I play some guitar and video games. I am also between cats at the moment, but once I'm settled down I would like to change that.
